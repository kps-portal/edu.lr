Scope of Access
Every scholarship and fee tier recorded here is real and visible to everyone with a reason to see it: the student themselves (their own record), the Registrar, and the Super Administrator. The Principal and DEO dashboards see the school-wide total value of active scholarships folded into the Fee Collections figure, with a labeled footnote — never hidden, never a separate untracked pool.
