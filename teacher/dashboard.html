Scope of Access
The Teacher role covers everything for the classes and subjects assigned to you — lesson planning, assignments, homework, quizzes, exams, practicals, projects, grading, attendance and reports. Academic structure, user accounts and system-wide settings remain with the Super Administrator.

Correction Requests

Review →
Teacher can: create lesson plans & notes, set assignments, homework, quizzes, exams, practicals and projects, record marks and grades, mark attendance, and submit reports and correction requests for your assigned classes and subjects.
Teacher cannot: create or edit user accounts, change academic structure (years, classes, sections, subjects), edit the grading system, or alter published records — those go through a Correction Request for Admin approval.
