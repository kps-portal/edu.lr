Maintenance & Remote Actions

Re-send Pending Notifications
Re-sends email and SMS notifications for any ticket update that failed to reach the reporter or the assigned technician.
Run SLA Compliance Scan
Checks every open ticket against its category’s response and resolution time targets and flags any breaches.
Re-sync Technician Assignments
Verifies that each ticket’s assigned technician matches current staff directory and on-call schedule records.

Danger Zone

These actions affect live tickets and reporter communications and cannot be undone remotely. Every attempt is logged with your account, IP address and timestamp.

Force-close a Ticket
Immediately closes a single ticket regardless of its current status and notifies the reporter. Enter the exact ticket ID to confirm.
Bulk Close Stale Tickets
Closes every ticket that has had no activity for more than 90 days and removes them from active queue counts.
Purge Entire Ticket Queue
Permanently deletes every ticket and its history from the helpdesk system. Only proceed if this is a planned, full reset of the support system — this cannot be recovered.
No sample data policy: every number, ticket row and alert on this page is loaded live from the Supabase database. Nothing is hardcoded, and destructive actions always require typed confirmation before they run.
