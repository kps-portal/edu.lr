Scope of Access
Every visit logged here is saved to your own DEO account and stays there until you act on it. A visit flagged as a Special visit (or any visit you decide needs escalation) can generate a pre-filled Special Report in one click — the report stays linked back to the visit that triggered it, and only becomes official once you press "Finalize Report" on the report itself.
