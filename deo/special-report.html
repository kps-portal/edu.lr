Scope of Access
Special Reports are ad hoc — investigations, incidents, or anything outside the regular monthly / quarterly / annual cycle. Unlike the Annual Report, you can keep as many as you need. Each report is saved to your own account the moment you save a draft, and only becomes official once you press Finalize Report.
