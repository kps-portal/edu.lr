What this page shows

Attendance is the ratio of membersPresent to membersTotal you enter for each meeting, shown as a coloured pill (green ≥ 80%, gold 60–79%, red below 60%) and rolled up into the Average Attendance Rate stat above.

Schools represented counts the number of distinct schools with at least one LSMC meeting on record out of the district's total schools in institutions — a coverage measure of committee participation district-wide, not an official LSMC registration count.

Schools are read live from the same institutions table used throughout the DEO portal. Meeting records are read and written directly through a dedicated lsmc_meetings table (foreign-keyed to institutions), visible to every DEO / Super Admin account district-wide — not scoped to whoever created the record.

DEO can: log an LSMC meeting for any school in the district, record attendance and key decisions, set and track follow-up actions to open/closed, edit or delete a meeting record, search/filter/sort the register, and export it to CSV.
