Mean Score by Subject (District-wide)

What this page shows

"Mean score" is the district-wide average of score ÷ max_score across every published mark, expressed as a percentage — not a normalized exam grade.

"Pass rate" is the share of published marks at or above 50% of the maximum possible score, matching the threshold already used in the district's own statistics pages.

This page does not yet break results down by examination sitting (e.g. First Semester vs Second Semester) or by grade level — see the handoff note for what a future pass could add using the same marks table.

DEO can: review already-published examination and assessment results across every school in the district, compare schools and subjects, and identify schools that may need academic support — all read-only.
