Scope of Access
The District Education Officer supervises schools, principals, teachers and learners within Careysburg District — scheduling and recording school visits and inspections, monitoring teaching & learning, curriculum coverage, facilities and resource needs, tracking compliance with district policies, escalating risks and safeguarding concerns, and preparing monthly, quarterly, annual and special reports for the district record. User accounts, system settings and each school's internal academic approvals remain under the Super Administrator's and Principal's control respectively.

Open Supervision Plan
DEO can: schedule and record school visits and inspections; monitor teaching & learning, curriculum coverage and assessment results; track facilities, resource needs, projects and district budget requests; participate in LSMC governance; track compliance and district policies; log complaints, incidents, risks and safeguarding actions; and prepare, finalize and print monthly, quarterly, annual and special reports.
DEO cannot: create or delete user accounts, change system settings, or approve internal school records such as results, report cards, promotions and graduation eligibility — these remain exclusive to each school's Super Administrator and Principal.
