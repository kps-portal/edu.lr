Scope of Access
This view reads one school's live record from public.institutions, joined against profiles (its principal and teaching staff), students (active learners, with class/section) and attendance_daily_summary (today plus the last 14 days). It is read-only: the DEO can supervise this school from here, but cannot create, edit or deactivate its institutions record — that remains the Super Administrator's responsibility.

What this profile shows

Identity & Contact fields (motto, type, established year, registration number, address, county, country, phone, email, website) are read directly from this school's institutions row. A field left off this list simply hasn't been set on file yet for this school — nothing here is invented.

Teaching Staff / Leadership / Learners are live rows in profiles and students whose institution_id matches this school — the same join used across the DEO's School Directory and Teacher Register.

Attendance is this school's own rows in attendance_daily_summary (present ÷ total marked each day). A day with nothing submitted is skipped, not shown as 0%.

A per-school Last Inspection date and classroom-visit history are intentionally not shown here: inspection_reports and classroom_visits have no institution_id column anywhere in this schema yet, so they cannot be honestly linked to a single school. See HANDOFF-school-profile.html.md before adding either.

DEO can: view this school's live identity, leadership, teaching staff, learner roster and attendance history, and jump straight into scheduling a visit, an inspection or a resource-needs review — all read-only.
