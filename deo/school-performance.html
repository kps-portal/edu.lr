Scope of Access
This scorecard reads every school in public.institutions, joined against profiles (principals), published rows in marks (via students) for an Academic score, and attendance_daily_summary for an Attendance score. It is read-only: the DEO can review scores and flag schools for improvement here, but cannot edit a mark, an attendance record or a school's profile — those stay the Teacher/Principal's and Super Administrator's responsibility respectively.

School Performance Scorecards and Improvement Priorities

Each school's Academic and Attendance scores, computed directly from already-published marks and submitted attendance, combined into an overall score used only to sort schools by improvement priority — not an officially certified rating.

What this page shows

Academic % is the average of (score ÷ max score) across every published row in marks belonging to that school's students — the same calculation statistics.html already runs. A school with no published marks yet shows "—", not 0%.

Attendance % is today's row in attendance_daily_summary for that school (present ÷ total marked). A school with no attendance submitted yet today shows "—", not 0%.

Overall Score is a UI-only heuristic — 60% Academic % + 40% Attendance % when both are on file, falling back to whichever one is on file, and left blank if neither is. It is not an officially certified rating.

Rating and Rank both come from Overall Score — Excelling ≥80, Satisfactory 60–79, Needs Improvement below 60, Pending with no data yet. Rank is each school's fixed position by Overall Score and does not change when you sort the table by a different column.

A school "Type" / "Level" column and a per-school "Last Inspection" column are intentionally not shown: inspection_reports has no institution_id link anywhere in this schema yet, so a per-school inspection date cannot be shown honestly. See the handoff note before inventing one.

DEO can: view every school's live Academic %, Attendance % and Overall Score, sort and filter by rating, export the scorecard to CSV, and identify schools that need improvement follow-up — all read-only.
