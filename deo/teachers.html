Scope of Access
This view reads the district's live teacher roster directly from public.profiles where role = 'teacher', joined against institutions for school placement and against class_subject_teachers (current academic year) for subject assignments. It is read-only: the DEO cannot create, edit, deactivate or reassign a teacher account here — account and deployment changes remain the Super Admin's and each school's Principal's responsibility.

What this page shows

"Active" reflects each account's is_active flag (and status where set) on profiles — the same flag every other portal in EDU.LR already uses to gate login.

"Subjects (Current Term)" comes from class_subject_teachers rows for the academic year currently marked is_current = true. A teacher with no rows there shows "Not yet assigned" — that is a real gap in scheduling, not a loading error.

Qualification, years of experience and teacher-attendance figures are not shown here because no live column or table for them exists yet in the schema — see the handoff note rather than trust placeholder numbers.

DEO can: view every teacher account and its school placement across the district, see current-term subject load, and identify schools that are unstaffed or thin on teachers — all read-only.
