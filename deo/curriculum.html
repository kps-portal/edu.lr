Scope of Access & What "Implementation" Means Here
This page reads live rows from subjects and classes (the national curriculum's master subject and grade-level catalog, set up by the Super Admin), class_subject_teachers (which subject is actually assigned to a teacher for a class, for the current academic year — the same table super-admin/subjects.html and super-admin/classes.html write to), and profiles/institutions (to attribute each assignment to a school via the teacher's institution_id). It measures subject-staffing coverage — which subjects on the national list actually have a teacher assigned per school — not day-to-day syllabus or lesson-pacing progress, since no scheme-of-work or lesson-coverage table exists in this schema yet. Read-only: assignments are made from the Super Admin / Principal consoles, not here.

What this page shows

Subjects Staffed (per school) = the number of distinct subjects from the national subjects catalog that have at least one class_subject_teachers assignment this academic year, where the assigned teacher's profiles.institution_id matches that school.

Grade Levels Staffed = distinct classes (grade levels) with at least one staffed subject assignment at that school.

Subjects Not Yet Staffed = the national subject count minus Subjects Staffed at that school — subjects with no teacher assigned there yet, this year.

Staffing Coverage = Subjects Staffed ÷ total subjects in the national catalog. This is a staffing/assignment measure, not a claim about how much of each syllabus has actually been taught — this schema has no scheme-of-work, lesson-pacing or topic-coverage table to measure that directly.

Grade Levels With No Subject Staffed lists classes rows that have zero class_subject_teachers assignments district-wide this year — a signal that an entire grade band may have no formally assigned teacher for any subject.

Classes, subjects and class_subject_teachers assignments are national/master data set up by the Super Admin and are not tagged with an institution_id directly; school attribution here is derived by joining each assignment's teacher_id to that teacher's profiles.institution_id, the same join deo/teachers.html already relies on. Assignments made through a section instead of a class-wide slot, or teachers with no institution_id on file, are grouped under a synthetic "Unassigned" row rather than silently dropped.

DEO can: view live subject-staffing coverage for every school in the district against the national curriculum catalog, see which grade levels or subjects are unstaffed, filter and export the school table — all read-only.
