Scope of Access
This view reads directly from public.audit_logs (joined to profiles for the actor's name and role) and public.login_history for sign-in activity — the same accountability tables super-admin/audit-log.html already uses. There is no per-district column on audit_logs in this schema, so this trail is system-wide, not filtered to Careysburg District alone. The DEO cannot edit, delete or annotate any entry here.

Audit & Accountability

Accountability Principles

Least privilege — users receive only the access needed for their duties.

Traceability — sensitive changes remain attributable and reviewable through this log.

Evidence integrity — reports and inspection evidence should be protected from silent alteration.

What this page shows

Each row is one row from audit_logs: who did what, to which record, and when. "Approvals" groups the workflow tables (approval_requests, promotions, suspensions, transfer_requests, graduation_requests, correction_requests); "User changes" is anything on profiles; everything else not a login/logout falls under "System".

Failed sign-in attempts come from login_history, not audit_logs, and are surfaced separately in the stat cards above.

DEO can: search, filter and export the district's accountability trail to investigate an incident or confirm an action was properly logged — all read-only.
